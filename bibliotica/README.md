# Bibliotica — Thesis Reference Library

Research topic: **Statistical Methods for Analyzing Price Dynamics and Trading Volumes in the Cryptocurrency Market**.

## Core books (APA-style references)

1. **Statistical methods** — Tsay, R. S. (2010). *Analysis of financial time series* (3rd ed.). Wiley. https://doi.org/10.1002/9780470644560
2. **Cryptocurrency market** — Burniske, C., & Tatar, J. (2017). *Cryptoassets: The innovative investor's guide to Bitcoin and beyond*. McGraw-Hill.
3. **Price dynamics** — Taylor, S. J. (2005). *Asset price dynamics, volatility, and prediction*. Princeton University Press.
4. **Trading volumes / market microstructure** — O'Hara, M. (1995). *Market microstructure theory*. Blackwell.
5. **Integrated financial statistics and cryptocurrencies** — Franke, J., Härdle, W. K., & Hafner, C. M. (2019). *Statistics of financial markets: An introduction*. Springer. https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-030-13751-9

## How these sources support the thesis

| Topic | Primary reference | Application |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Statistical methods | Tsay (2010) | ARIMA, GARCH, diagnostics, forecasting |
| Cryptocurrency market | Burniske & Tatar (2017) | Market background, cryptoasset classification |
| Price dynamics | Taylor (2005) | Return processes, volatility, prediction |
| Trading volumes | O'Hara (1995) | Liquidity, trading mechanisms, price formation |
| Integrated framework | Franke et al. (2019) | Financial econometrics and cryptocurrency-specific analysis |

**Scope note:** O'Hara (1995) provides market microstructure theory rather than a dedicated cryptocurrency-volume model. Franke et al. (2019) covers cryptocurrency econometrics but is not a comprehensive treatment of trading volume. Supplement these books with recent empirical studies on crypto price–volume relationships, especially for the 2018–2025 daily-data sample.

These are bibliographic references and publisher links, not copyrighted book files.
